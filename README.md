## 🧭 About Me

I'm an MBA in Computer Management professional interested in the intersection of **financial markets, data analytics, research, and technology**.

My experience in financial-market research has strengthened my ability to investigate information, verify data against reliable sources, understand market developments, and communicate findings clearly.

I enjoy working with datasets, exploring market trends, building dashboards, and turning raw information into structured, useful insights.

- 📊 **Analytics:** Exploring stock performance, sector trends, risk, and returns.
- 📈 **Financial Research:** Equity markets, IPOs, company filings, and derivatives.
- 🛠️ **Tools:** Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, and data visualization.
- 🎓 **Education:** MBA in Computer Management & IT.
- 📜 **Certifications:** NISM Series XV – Research Analyst and NISM Series VIII – Equity Derivatives.
- 🌱 **Currently building:** Financial data analysis projects and interactive market dashboards.
